# Writing the script and storyboard

Write with the user, short and concrete. A video that works is a small story, not a list of pretty shots.

## Before any prompt
Settle with the user (ask only what is missing):
- **Length and format**: duration and aspect (e.g. 10s, 9:16 for reels/shorts, 16:9 for YouTube). Matches `format` in the config.
- **Premise in one sentence** with a setup, a turn and a payoff. One idea per clip.
- **Cast**: which saved Characters appear (exact names, as saved in Flow). Do not invent characters that are not saved.
- **Setting and look**: where, time of day, camera style, colour/mood. Keep it identical across clips for continuity.
- **Sound**: dialogue, music, or silent with a caption.

## Shape of a short
- 0-2s hook: something happening or a question, never a slow establishing shot.
- Middle: one clear action or change, visible without sound.
- Last 2s: payoff or reveal, then stop. Leave room for a caption.

## Per-clip description (what you give Flow's agent)
One block per clip:
1. Who is on screen (Character names) and what each does, in order.
2. Where, and the camera (static, slow push-in, handheld, wide/close).
3. Expression and body language at the key moment (be specific; "smiles" is too vague).
4. What changes from the previous clip (continuity: same outfit, props, light).
5. Duration of the clip.

Prefer plain description to prompt-speak. Ask Flow's agent for staging advice first, react to a concrete suggestion, then ask for a storyboard, then the clips. Read what it actually produced before moving on; ask for focused fixes instead of regenerating everything.

## Captions
Write the caption text with the script: one short line (max ~8 words per line, 2-3 lines at most), what a viewer needs without sound. Check the rendered frame afterwards (placement, contrast, not covering faces).

## Rights and safety
Original characters and scenes only, or ones the user owns or has rights to. No real people's likeness without permission. If Flow refuses on policy grounds, tell the user; do not try to word your way around the refusal.
